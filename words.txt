# Class list. One word per line. A meaning can follow after a tab or a | sign (it is optional).
bird	An animal with feathers and wings. Most birds can fly. Birds lay eggs.
mammal	An animal with fur or hair. A mother mammal feeds her baby with milk. Dogs and cats are mammals.
amphibian	An animal that lives in water and on land. Frogs are amphibians.
reptile	An animal with dry, hard skin (scales). Snakes and crocodiles are reptiles.
insect	A very small animal with six legs. Ants and bees are insects.
fish	An animal that lives in water. It swims and has fins.
penguin	A black and white bird. It cannot fly, but it can swim very well. It lives in cold places.
chick	A baby bird.
colony	A big group of animals that live together. A penguin colony has many penguins.
huddle	When animals or people stand close together to stay warm.
hatch	When a baby bird comes out of its egg.
breed	When animals make babies.
mate	When a male and a female animal come together to have babies.
stone	A small, hard piece of rock.
protect	To keep someone or something safe.
temperature	How hot or cold something is. Example: "The temperature is 30 degrees."
freezing	Very, very cold. Water turns to ice.
