Keyboard shortcuts
  • Start screen: 1 to 6 choose a level, + or − change the number of words, W word sound, L letters, M meaning and B boxes on or off, Enter to start.
  • During a test: Up arrow hears the word, Down arrow spells it out, Enter shows the word and then goes to the next word, Esc goes home.
  • Score page: Enter plays again, S saves the picture, H or Esc goes back to the start.
